Our Story

DTB Technologies began as a small IT consultancy in Nairobi, helping local businesses get online and stay running. Today we're based in Sydney, Australia, serving clients across Australia, Kenya and beyond — building custom websites, mobile and desktop apps, and the hosting that keeps it all online. Different scale, same principles: solid software, honest work, clear pricing.

Solid software, built properly, delivered on time.
