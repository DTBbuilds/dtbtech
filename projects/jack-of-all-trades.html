Jack of All Trades

Community Job Marketplace

A mobile-first marketplace that helps communities post available work, discover opportunities and coordinate jobs more clearly than a busy group chat.

What It Does

Post Available Work

Community members can post jobs and available work in one place, instead of scattering requests across group chats.

Discover Opportunities

Workers and tradespeople can browse and discover local opportunities from a single, organised feed.

Coordinate Jobs Clearly

Built to make job coordination clearer and more reliable than a busy community group chat.

Currently in Private Beta

Jack of All Trades is our latest build and is being tested with a private group before a wider release. Interested in early access or a similar platform for your community? Talk to us.
