Tradie Website FAQs

I already have a website. Is this for me?

Yes. Website Rescue starts from A$450 for a clearly scoped repair, not a complete redesign. We review the issue and agree the work before starting.

I don't have a website yet. Where should I start?

Get Online starts from A$990 for a focused one-page site. For service pages, work examples and a fuller quote-request journey, Win More Enquiries starts from A$2,490.

How much does a tradie website cost?

Website Rescue starts from A$450, Get Online from A$990, Win More Enquiries from A$2,490 and Run Your Crew from A$4,990. These are indicative one-off project starting prices in AUD. The written quote confirms scope, GST treatment, hosting and separate software costs.

Does Run Your Crew include a custom staff portal?

Run Your Crew sets up a staff-accessible workspace in a compatible third-party job-management platform; licence fees are separate. A custom DTB Crew Portal with its own staff login, assignments and job-status workflow is a separately quoted software project, starting from A$7,900 for a narrow MVP.

Will my website rank first on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee rankings, enquiries or booked jobs. DTB provides relevant on-page foundations, mobile usability and clear contact routes.

How does the free website check work?

The short form prepares a WhatsApp or email draft that you review and send. It does not automatically submit or store your details. We then discuss a practical next step.

Do you only work with tradies in Sydney?

DTB Technologies is based in Sydney and works with tradies and local service businesses across Australia. Most of the work can be delivered remotely.
