Tradie Website FAQs

I already have a website. Is this for me?

Yes. Website Rescue is for existing sites that are slow, broken, outdated or hard to use on phones. We review what you have, identify the highest-impact fixes and quote the repair before any work starts.

I don't have a website yet. Where should I start?

The Tradie Lead Website is the usual starting point: a new mobile-first website with click-to-call, WhatsApp and a quote-request form, designed so customers can contact you easily from any device.

How much does a tradie website cost?

Website Rescue starts from A$450 and a Tradie Lead Website starts from A$1,500. The Local Lead Foundation is quoted per project. These are starting points: final pricing depends on scope and is confirmed in a written quote before work begins.

Will my website rank first on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee rankings. We build the technical foundations — clean markup, metadata, mobile speed, service pages and Google Business Profile alignment — that make your site indexable and easy for local customers to find.

How does the free website check work?

Send us your website through the short form on this page and we'll identify the three most important improvements — no obligation. The form prepares a WhatsApp or email message that you review and send yourself; nothing is automatically submitted or stored.

Do you only work with tradies in Sydney?

DTB Technologies is based in Sydney and works with tradies and local service businesses across Australia. Most of the work — builds, reviews and support — happens online, so location is rarely a barrier.
