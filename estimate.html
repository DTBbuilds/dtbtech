      How the range is prepared

      The AI feature, when enabled and consented to, suggests a project category, pages, staff count and features. A separate controlled pricing engine uses a dated DTB pricing profile to calculate an illustrative range. It is not a live market feed. A person at DTB must confirm requirements, delivery costs, GST and contractual terms before issuing a quote.
