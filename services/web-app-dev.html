FAQs

How long does a web application take to build?

A focused internal tool or dashboard typically takes 4–8 weeks. Larger portals, booking systems and marketplaces take longer depending on scope. We agree milestones up front.

Do you provide maintenance?

Yes. We offer flexible maintenance plans covering updates, backups, monitoring, and enhancements.

Can you integrate payments or other systems?

Yes. We integrate payment providers such as M-Pesa, Stripe and PayPal, and connect web apps to your existing tools via APIs.

Do you also build regular websites?

Yes — if you need a marketing or brochure site rather than an application, see our website development service.
