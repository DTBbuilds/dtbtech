What can custom business software solve?

When customer enquiries, job updates or stock records move between spreadsheets and disconnected apps, important work becomes harder to track. DTB can design a focused tool around the steps your team actually follows. We start by understanding the problem rather than assuming a large new platform is necessary.

Staff dashboards and job tracking

Bring job requests, assignments and status updates into one shared workspace. Roles and permissions are agreed during scoping.

Quoting and customer enquiries

Capture relevant project details, organise follow-up and connect enquiry records to a suitable customer-management process.

Product, stock and system integration

Create product catalogues, inventory views and APIs that connect approved tools, where integration is practical and secure.

See the scope of our web apps and staff portals, the KEUS Logistics catalogue and enquiry website, and our own Jack of All Trades marketplace project. These are examples of different kinds of work, not promises of identical features in every new project.

Questions before commissioning custom software

Should we use an existing platform or build our own?

Start by checking whether a reliable existing tool already meets your needs. A custom application makes more sense when your roles, approvals, reporting or integrations cannot be handled well by that tool. DTB can help compare those approaches before recommending a build.

What changes the cost of a business software project?

User roles, data migration, integrations, payment handling, reports, security requirements and testing all affect scope. A small, well-defined first release can be easier to budget than attempting every feature at once. SmartQuote offers a nonbinding planning estimate; a written quote follows a scope discussion.
