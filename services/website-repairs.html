Frequently Asked Questions

Do you only fix websites originally built by DTB?

No. We can assess an existing website built by another team. Whether we can safely change it depends on its platform, access, dependencies and condition.

Will I need a completely new website?

Not necessarily. We review the existing site and can recommend a focused repair, a phased refresh or a rebuild when appropriate.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the platform and issue. After discussing the scope, we'll provide a quote showing what's included before any paid work starts.

How do I request help?

Use the project brief to prepare a WhatsApp or email message. You'll review and send it yourself; the current form does not automatically submit or save information.
