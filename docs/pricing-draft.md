# DRAFT — Starter offer & pricing framework (NOT FOR PUBLICATION)

Status: **Draft for owner sign-off** — DTB-CONVERT-001C deliverable.
Nothing here is published. Do not put these figures on the site until scope,
inclusions, revisions, hosting, GST/tax treatment and payment milestones are
confirmed in writing by the owner.

## Purpose

Give a first-time Sydney SMB visitor enough certainty to request a quote,
without locking DTB into unscoped promises. Recommended public pattern:
**"from $X" starting points + what's included + a written-quote step** —
fixed prices only where scope is truly fixed.

## Decisions needed from owner before publishing

1. Which offers get public "from" prices vs "scoped per project".
2. GST handling: registered? prices incl./excl. GST wording.
3. Revisions included per tier (e.g. 2 rounds) and cost of extras.
4. Payment milestones (e.g. 50% start / 50% launch — or 40/40/20).
5. Hosting & care plan: bundled months, monthly price, what's included,
   and whether it's optional (recommended: optional, clearly separable).
6. Support scope wording for the site: response time, channels, exclusions.
7. Currency display for Kenya-facing work (AUD primary; KES on request?).
