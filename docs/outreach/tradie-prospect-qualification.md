# Western Sydney tradie prospect qualification

**Use only evidence from an official, currently accessible business site or a separately verified published business listing.** An ugly site is not inherently broken; no booking form is not proof that the business wants online booking.

## Screening gates

- Business appears operational in Sydney/Western Sydney; sector matches plumbers, electricians, cleaners or painters.
- An official business site or verified official listing exists and is recorded with its URL and review date.
- At least one published **business-owned** contact route exists (website form, business phone, or business email); do not capture private employee contact details in a public issue.
- A plausible, small, deliverable DTB service can be described without speculative claims.
- Respect no-solicitation language, unsubscribes, and the business's preferred channel. Australian spam/privacy requirements must be checked before electronic marketing; a published address alone does not establish marketing consent.

## What qualifies as evidence

| Label | Example | What to log privately |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observed defect | Contact link returns an error or misroutes | Exact page/link, test date, device, result |
| Observed friction | Mobile navigation hides the contact button behind several steps | Reproducible path and screenshots without personal data |
| Optional improvement | Call-only enquiry flow could optionally offer a quote request | Current route and a clearly hypothetical suggestion |
| Not verified | Site blocked, timed out, or a test could not be completed | Mark NOT VERIFIED; do not assert a problem |

Never claim slow page speed without a dated measurement/method, or poor search rankings or missing sales without defensible evidence.

## Score (four dimensions, each 1–5; maximum 20)

| Factor | 1 | 3 | 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Verified website friction | No validated problem | Minor reproducible friction | Clear user-impacting, reproducible defect |
| Service fit | Unclear match | Useful optional improvement | Narrow DTB repair, contact or booking workflow fit |
| Official contact pathway | No validated legitimate path | Reachable but indirect | Clearly published relevant business channel |
| Scope/deliverability | Unclear or large unbounded rebuild | Some scope questions | Specific small engagement DTB can quote responsibly |

**Prioritisation:** 16–20 high fit; 12–15 research or clarify; 8–11 defer; 4–7 reject. Never invent precise scores in advance. Weights are equal and can be revised after recording actual outcomes.
