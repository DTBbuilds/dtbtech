# Three-point website check — internal template

A mini-audit is helpful only if it is **specific, correct, dated and respectful**. This is not a security penetration test and must not access private areas or submit a company's live forms merely to test them.

## Before writing

- Check the official business website URL and current published services.
- Verify each observation yourself; note the URL, local date, viewport and steps.
- Check mobile menu and enquiry/contact path on a normal browser without creating a real booking.
- Do not describe website speed, ranking, conversion losses or business revenue without genuine measurement.
- Separate observations from suggestions. It is acceptable to find no actionable problems and skip outreach.

## Quality rules

- No invented testimonials, lead volumes, ranks, site performance scores or claimed business relationships.
- No screenshots containing passwords, private messages, customer details or other sensitive data.
- Never claim a customer *needs* online booking; offer it as an option that can be discussed.
- Do not promise prices/turnaround beyond approved public offer descriptions and a written scope.
- Keep the full audit private; use **one concise verified point** in a first-contact message, not a critique of the business.
