## Positioning

DTB Technologies helps Australian small businesses (especially tradies and local services) move through three stages: **Get Online → Win More Enquiries → Run Your Crew**. Use a simple repair offer as a low-risk entry point and offer custom portal development only when existing job-management tools are insufficient.

Avoid generic Starter / Business / Premium labels or unsupported sales claims. Name the business outcome and specify the scope.

### What the staff portal is — and isn't

**Run Your Crew** is a website plus setup of an existing tool (for example, ServiceM8 or Tradify where suitable). We configure staff access and a job flow. Don't claim a custom branded DTB portal or automatic two-way sync. Confirm supported provider integrations/links and license limits on each project before estimating.

**DTB Crew Portal** is a separately designed web app, suitable when normal off-the-shelf job-management software does not fit. Starting price is for a bounded MVP with limited roles and core job tracking. No payroll, rostering rules engine, advanced GPS dispatch, accounting, payment processing, native mobile app, enterprise SSO, or complex offline mode in that entry scope. Plan for security, monitoring and maintenance as a separate contract.

### Customer-facing inclusions and limitations

- One-off project fees (no recurring software or hosting implicitly included).
- Actual price depends on pages, copy/assets, integrations, third-party API terms, existing site quality and delivery complexity.
- Final written quote states GST treatment based on DTB's actual registration, inclusions/exclusions, delivery phases, revision rounds, support handover and third-party charges. Never show tax treatment that hasn't been verified.
- No guarantee of search rankings, lead volume, booked jobs, profitability or app uptime.
- No live enquiry/booking/notification claim until real delivery is tested for that client's setup.
- Copy, domain, business email, hosting, paid themes, stock media, ads and ongoing management are separate unless specifically itemised.

## Sales process

1. Ask what is currently costing the business time: poor contact route, unclear offering, missed enquiries, staff coordination.
2. Identify customer type, services, work examples/assets and current website; choose a package starting point rather than upselling blindly.
3. For staff workflows, ask staff count, jobs per month, devices, what they need to view/update and current job software.
4. Decide whether an existing platform is suitable. Use a custom portal discovery/estimate only when there's genuine workflow differentiation.
5. Give a written breakdown of project fee, optional care, and third-party subscriptions. Confirm ownership, acceptance criteria and data security requirements.
